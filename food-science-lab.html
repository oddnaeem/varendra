What Is Food?

Food is any edible substance that supplies the body with energy and nutrients — essential for life, body function, and growth.

Provides

Energy

Supports

Station 10 — Blanching Lab · Signature Instrument

Run the Enzyme Activity Simulator

Blanching is a mild heat treatment that inactivates the enzymes that spoil color, flavor, and texture during storage. Drag the temperature and watch enzyme activity respond.

Enzyme Activity Monitor

37°C
0% activity100% activity
Recommended blanch time 2–3 minutes

Enzymes on the Bench

Polyphenol OxidaseBrowning (apple, potato, banana)
ChlorophyllaseFades green (spinach, peas)
LipoxygenaseOff-flavor (soybean, corn)
PolygalacturonaseSoftens fruit (tomato)
PeroxidaseColor & flavor loss (most produce)
Method

Hot Water Blanching

85–100°C immersion. Simple, low-cost — but leaches water-soluble vitamins and creates wastewater.

Method

Steam Blanching

Better vitamin retention and flavor, no wastewater — but needs pricier equipment and more time.
